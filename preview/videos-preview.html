Adding a video takes one field

Paste the YouTube or Vimeo link. The title, thumbnail and player all follow from it — nothing is uploaded and no cover is needed.

The panel shows you the thumbnail as you paste, so a wrong link is obvious before you publish.

Filed the same way as documents

One type, one to three themes led by the four Ps, and free keywords. Staff learn the system once.

Types here are video-specific: awareness, testimony, training, event highlights, interview, reflection, documentary.

The sharpest frame YouTube has

The card asks for the 1280×720 still first and steps down through the smaller sizes only if that video has none, so nothing is upscaled and nothing arrives letterboxed.

A coloured cover is drawn underneath and the photograph fades in on top, so a slow connection never shows a black box.

Why it stays fast

A video is a link, so the page carries only thumbnails. The player iframe is created on the tap that plays it and destroyed on close.

YouTube is loaded from its no-cookie host, so nothing tracks a visitor who never presses play.

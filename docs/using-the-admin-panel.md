# Updating the website — a guide for staff

This is the everyday guide. You do not need to know anything technical, and
you cannot break the website by clicking around: nothing you do is visible to
the public until you press **Publish**.

For the one-time technical setup (tokens, environment variables), see
[admin-panel.md](admin-panel.md) instead.

---

## Getting in

**Go to:** `https://talithakumraht.org/admin`

Type it straight into the address bar, after the website address. There is no
link to it from the public site — that is deliberate, so visitors never find
it. Bookmark it once you are in.

Sign in with your **email address and password**. These are specific to this
website; they are not your Gmail, and not the Talitha Kum email account.

> **The very first time anyone ever opens it,** the panel asks you to create
> the first account instead of signing in. Whoever does this becomes the
> administrator. This happens once, for the whole organisation.

### If it says your password is wrong

Try again carefully — passwords are case-sensitive. If it still refuses, ask
the administrator (whoever set up the panel) to set a new password for you
from the **Users** screen. There is no self-service password reset, and no
"forgot password" email.

### If you see "Your session has ended"

Normal. You stay signed in for seven days, then it asks again. Sign back in
and carry on.

## What you will see

**Home** is where you land. It shows how much of each kind of content the
website holds, what was published most recently, and a button to open the
live site in a new tab.

Down the left (or, on a phone, in the bar across the top) are the sections you
are allowed to change:

| Section | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **News** | Stories on the News page. The newest three also appear on the homepage. |
| **Publications** | Reports, briefs and manuals people can read or download. |
| **Videos** | Films played from YouTube or Vimeo. |
| **Team members** | The board and staff on the Our Team page. |
| **Photo gallery** | Photographs on the Gallery page. |

You may not see all of these. Each account is given only the sections it
needs. If a section you expect is missing, an administrator can add it.

## Adding or changing something

1. Open the section, for example **News**.
2. Press **+ New story** — or click any existing item to change it.
3. Fill in the form. Fields marked with a red **\*** are required.
4. Press **Publish**.

You will then see a green **Published** panel confirming it worked, with a
button to view it on the live website. That panel stays until you move on —
it is not a message that disappears.

**Changes take about a minute to appear on the public website.** The site
rebuilds itself after every publish. If you look immediately and do not see
your change, wait a minute and refresh the page.

### Photographs

Press **Choose…** to pick a picture from your computer or phone. Files must
be **under about 4 MB** — most phone photos are larger, so you may need to
resize one first. If a photo is rejected for being too big, WhatsApp it to
yourself and save the copy: WhatsApp shrinks it automatically.

### Writing a story

The **story** box accepts simple formatting:

- Leave a blank line between paragraphs
- `**like this**` makes text bold
- A line starting with `-` becomes a bullet point

### Leaving without publishing

If you have typed something and try to navigate away, the panel asks whether
you really want to lose it. If you say yes, the work is gone — there is no
draft saved anywhere.

## The photo gallery

The gallery works slightly differently, because photographs are usually added
in batches:

1. Press **+ Add photos** and select as many as you like at once.
2. Give each one a caption. **Please do write captions** — they are read aloud
   to blind visitors using screen readers, and they help the photographs show
   up in Google.
3. Reorder with the **←** and **→** buttons. The first photographs are the
   ones that appear on the homepage.
4. Press **Publish gallery**.

Adding, reordering, captioning and removing all do nothing until you press
**Publish gallery**. Until then you can change your mind freely.

## Managing people (administrators only)

**Users** lists everyone who can sign in.

**To add someone:** press **+ Add user**, enter their name, email and a
starting password, choose their role, and untick any sections they should not
change. Tell them the email and password yourself — the panel does not send
any email.

**To reset a forgotten password:** open the person, type a new password, and
save. Leave the password box empty if you only want to change something else.

**Roles:**

- **Editor** — only the sections you tick for them
- **Administrator** — everything, including managing other people

Administrators always have every section, so the section boxes are greyed out
when you choose that role.

You cannot remove your own account or demote yourself, so the website can
never end up with nobody in charge.

## If something goes wrong

**"Something with a very similar name already exists here."**
Two items whose titles are nearly identical would end up at the same web
address. Change the title a little — add the year, or the place.

**"Could not reach the server."**
Your internet connection dropped. Check it and try again; nothing was lost.

**A photo will not upload.**
It is over 4 MB. Shrink it, or send it via WhatsApp and use that copy.

**I published but the website has not changed.**
Wait a minute, then refresh with **Ctrl+Shift+R** (or **Cmd+Shift+R** on a
Mac). Browsers hold on to old copies of pages.

**I renamed a story — will old links break?**
No. Each story keeps the web address it was first published at, even if you
correct the headline later, so links already shared on WhatsApp or Facebook
keep working.

---

## Things worth knowing

- **Nothing is ever silently overwritten.** If you try to create something
  that would replace an existing item, the panel refuses and explains why.
- **Every change is recorded.** Any earlier version of the website can be
  restored exactly, so a mistake is always recoverable — ask whoever
  maintains the site.
- **The panel works on a phone**, though writing a long story is easier on a
  computer.
- **Sign out** when using a shared or public computer.

Vision, Mission & Values

What guides everything we do

Three commitments, held by every congregation and partner in the network.

Our Vision

Inspired by the mercy of God, we envision a world free from human trafficking.

Our Mission

To uphold human dignity at all costs — from modern-day slavery to freedom — collaborating to eradicate human trafficking.

Our Values

Unity in Diversity Mutual Support Integrity Honesty Confidentiality Prayer

Walk with us

Every value on this page is lived out by people — in shelters, parishes, schools and border towns across Kenya. Join them.

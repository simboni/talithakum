Talitha Kum Kenya

Where shattered lives find hope, and resilience blossoms into freedom

Since 2016, a network of consecrated religious, lay women and men has worked across Kenya to prevent human trafficking, protect survivors and restore dignity — through prayer, protection, prevention and partnership.

Since the Jubilee Year of Mercy

Talitha Kum International — Kenya

In 2016, Talitha Kum Kenya began during the Jubilee Year of Mercy, initiated by a collective effort of consecrated religious, lay women and men. Inspired by Pope Francis’ call to embody the corporal works of mercy, the organisation reaches out to those who live on the margins of society.

“Human trafficking is a crime against humanity. We must unite our efforts to free victims and stop this crime that’s become ever more aggressive, that threatens not just individuals, but the foundational values of society.” — Pope Francis
